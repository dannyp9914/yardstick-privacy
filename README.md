# yardstick-privacy
Privacy policy for the Yardstick app

**Effective date:** 30 September 2026

Yardstick does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The app uses your device's camera only to display augmented reality measurements in real time. No images, videos, or location data are collected or stored.

Measurements you choose to save are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded or shared, and you can delete them at any time from the Measurements list in the app. Deleting the app removes them.

Yardstick makes no network connections. No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking tools are included.
